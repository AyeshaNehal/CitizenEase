# Sources Log & Verification Register

All facts, requirements, office locations, and fee guidelines presented to Karachi citizens must be logged in this register.

## Verification Status Levels
- **`verified`**: Confirmed on an official government portal (`.gov.pk`), published gazette, official court ruling, or documented DC office visit.
- **`unverified`**: Lead gathered from news articles, blogs, community forums, or unofficial guide sites. Requires verification by Member 4.
- **`outdated`**: Source verified over 90 days ago or superseded by new regulations (e.g. 2025/2026 NADRA biometric updates).
- **`conflicting`**: Multiple sources disagree (e.g. photo quantity, address matching). Must show "confirm with office" banner.
